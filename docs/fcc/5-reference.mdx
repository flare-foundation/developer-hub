**Flare Confidential Compute (FCC)** is the Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) protocol on Flare.
On-chain it is one [EIP-2535](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2535) diamond, `FlareTeeManager`.

Call the diamond at the `FlareTeeManager` address.
[`IFlareTeeManager`](/fcc/reference/IFlareTeeManager) is the aggregate interface for that address.
Facet interfaces are compile-time views of the same diamond — they have no extra deployment.

:::info
FCC smart contracts are available on **Coston2**, **Coston**, and **Songbird** today.
They will be available on other Flare networks soon.
Resolve `FlareTeeManager` at runtime through the [`FlareContractRegistry`](/network/guides/flare-contracts-registry) rather than hardcoding addresses once deployed on mainnet.
:::
