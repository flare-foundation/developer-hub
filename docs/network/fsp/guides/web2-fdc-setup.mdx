The [`Web2Json`](/fdc/attestation-types/web2-json) attestation type has no configured source on Flare Mainnet or Songbird.
[`FdcHub.requestAttestation`](/fdc/reference/IFdcHub#requestattestation) reverts for `Web2Json` on those networks.

## What providers should do

With no remaining `Web2Json` sources on Flare Mainnet or Songbird, the `web2-verifier` service has nothing further to serve.

Follow-up [`flare-systems-deployment`](https://github.com/flare-foundation/flare-systems-deployment) and [`fdc-suite-deployment`](https://github.com/flare-foundation/fdc-suite-deployment) releases will remove the configuration entries and the service definition.

Remove any remaining Web2Json source or verifier settings from your FDC client and verifier deployment.
Those settings no longer correspond to a live source.

## Testnets

`Web2Json` remains available on **Coston and Coston2** with the `PublicWeb2` source.
Developer guides use that source; see the [Web2Json specification](/fdc/attestation-types/web2-json).

Any future Web2 attestation source on Flare Mainnet or Songbird would be proposed separately under [FIP.14](https://proposals.flare.network/FIP/FIP_14.html#31-process-of-adding-updating-or-removing-an-api-endpoint).
